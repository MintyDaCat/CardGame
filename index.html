Poker practice

Texas Hold’em against bots. Play chips only: everyone starts with 1000 and your stack carries over.

2 to 23 players. Changing these settings restarts the game. Chips carry over from hand to hand; players who run out sit out.

Train the neural network

Training is heads-up (you versus one bot). In larger games the bots shrink their hand estimates to allow for extra opponents. The network starts by copying the rule bots, then improves by playing thousands of hands (self-play and against the bots). It runs in your browser and the result is saved on this device.
